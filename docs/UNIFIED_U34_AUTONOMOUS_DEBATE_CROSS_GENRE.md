## Original authored teaching and read-only source boundaries

- \`app/src/core/unified/u34-debate.ts\` uses the **existing** U33/U32 original-source-qualified lesson, witness and revision identities. It does not replicate canonical curriculum, introduce source importers, write assessments or create an Account store.
- **13 substantive source-bounded editorial debate briefs** across A1, A2, B1, B1+ and early B2 target real original lessons: A1 greetings and group identity; A2 unfinished film, housing and hotel requests; B1 transport scheduling, hybrid consultation and official document authority; advanced mediation, hedged translation, captioning rights, voluntary survey limitations and meteor observation uncertainty. Each brief includes a fair counterargument, an overclaim to avoid, authored source-specific misconception correction, natural French illustrative reply and a conditional verification constraint.
- Original, qualified **source witnesses** remain separate from U34 fictional editor/opponent remarks and newly authored model responses. The U34 model exposes original source locators, not new published source IDs or synthetic quotations.
- Each eligible original dialogue lesson offers **five progressively autonomous debate rounds** (thesis/source; civil opposition; reasoned concession; attribution repair; independent public synthesis). The sources anchor a structured **claim / original evidence / fair counterclaim / rebuttal / limitation** map, with visible authorial attribution and source limitations.
- **Four genuinely distinct output genres**: courteous service letter/email, evidence-weighted neutral analytic report, public spoken briefing (without recording), and editorial argument. Each provides genre-specific audience, composition structure, authentic source pointers and newly authored, explicitly labeled example. The source scopes remain unchanged as the learner adapts register.
- Each curated brief offers two independent source-bound, multiple-choice decisions with **specific misconception repair**, plus a third source-attribution decision when genuinely distinct original witnesses exist. They reuse the U23→U33 original source authorities and do not pretend to evaluate the semantics of open French output.
- Source comparisons prefer **two distinct original independently attributed written records**; otherwise transparently label an original reading and document, dialogue and document, or two turns of the **same** dialogue. A single dialogue is never claimed to contain two independent publications.

## Learner-facing functionality

\`app/src/routes/u34-debate-panel.ts\` + \`.css\` implement an interactive and recoverable **within-page** learning experience:

1. Source-bound claim/evidence/counterclaim maps with expandable actual original French witnesses.
2. Five-round adversarial-but-civil debate with keyboard-operable rounds, optional graduated strategy → authentic source → authored reply support, selectable peer/service/public register, reversible previous/next rounds, specific evidence limits, and independently authored French response space.
3. A separately drafted second response with the earlier draft and teaching scaffolds hidden. Explicit captured first version, later revision, side-by-side comparison and user-controlled clearing; **no automatic semantic improvement or oral accuracy grade**.
4. Four cross-genre drafting workspaces. Every genre retains its own temporary original and independent text while navigating among genres; the display does not silently lose drafts on round or genre change.
5. Genuine paired-source comparison with three independent original-source-specific notes, keyed by **both original witness locators** rather than a generic single note shared among unrelated documents.
6. Corrective original-source authored decisions, optional original evidence reveal, explicit provenance and misconception-specific feedback. Matches are narrow authored text-key comparisons only.
7. Responsive accessible writing/reading presentation with conventional rectangular controls, keyboard-visible focus and mobile one-column content.
8. Direct original Course, Speak, Listen, Read, Write and native scheduled Review navigation via the established U33/U32 original pathways; no new source routing or learner writer.

The U34 workspace is embedded into \`app/src/routes/u33-editorial-panel.ts\` as a child of the established U33/U32/U31 oral learning route. U34's local temporary-draft flag feeds U33's existing unsent-work signal and parent navigation safeguards. On cleanup the temporary state is discarded. This is **same mounted-page continuity only**, not IndexedDB or recovery after reload.
