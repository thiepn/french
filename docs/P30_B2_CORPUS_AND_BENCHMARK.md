# P30 — B2 Communicative Corpus Expansion, Advanced Functional Coverage & Benchmark Unlock

French 5.21.0 removes the former structural B2 ceiling.

The goal is not to label ordinary B1 tasks as B2. P30 adds a distinct upper-intermediate communicative corpus focused on reasoning, qualification, negotiation, mediation, and synthesis.

## New B2 corpus

### Reading and listening

P30 adds six original B2 texts:

1. **Réorganiser le travail hybride** — evaluating a workplace policy and its conditions.
2. **Densifier sans dégrader le quartier** — balancing housing, affordability, and neighborhood quality.
3. **Quand un algorithme recommande à notre place** — distinguishing reliability, bias, prediction, and social judgment.
4. **Prévenir plutôt que seulement réparer** — evaluating prevention, cost, and evidence.
5. **Rendre la mobilité plus équitable** — balancing transport restrictions and realistic alternatives.
6. **Décider quand les priorités s'opposent** — weighing competing priorities and reaching a compromise.

The existing P15 listening engine automatically generates one listening item from every reading, so these six texts create six B2 listening tasks without introducing a second content source.

Each text contains bilingual sentence support and retrieval questions, but reading/listening exposure still does not become vocabulary mastery automatically.

## Advanced communicative functions

P30 extends the P20 function taxonomy with:

- **Evaluate evidence / options**
- **Qualify a claim**
- **Explore consequences**
- **Build a persuasive case**
- **Mediate viewpoints**
- **Synthesize & conclude**

Each new function appears in at least two independent B2 conversation contexts. This avoids a function being considered covered from one memorized scenario.

## New B2 conversation scenarios

Five multi-turn scenarios are added:

- **Defend a workplace policy**
- **Debate a city transport policy**
- **Resolve a project crisis**
- **Assess a disputed media claim**
- **Mediate a team conflict**

B2 responses use a larger minimum-response target than B1 and are recorded as B2 evidence throughout the conversation and P20 curriculum systems.

The adaptive communicative curriculum can now reach B2 after substantial vocabulary exposure instead of being permanently capped at B1.

## New functional missions

Two B2 mission batteries combine the new scenarios:

### Advanced workplace decision

1. Resolve a project crisis
2. Defend a workplace policy
3. Mediate a team conflict

### Contested public issue

1. Assess a disputed media claim
2. Debate a city transport policy
3. Mediate a team conflict

The overlap is intentional: mediation is tested in both professional and public-reasoning batteries while the surrounding task chain changes.

## P25 progression

The old P25 invariant that B2 must remain structurally blocked has been removed.

B2 still does **not** become promoted automatically. It must satisfy the existing seven independent P25 gates:

- lexical retention;
- active transfer;
- reading;
- listening;
- speaking;
- interaction;
- functional missions.

It also still requires all prerequisite CEFR promotions.

P30 raises explicit B2 evidence-volume expectations for speaking and interaction rather than allowing B2 to inherit B1 defaults.

## P28 B2 benchmark

P28 now contains a real B2 benchmark:

- required missions: both new B2 mission batteries;
- minimum independent successful turns: **88%**;
- minimum calibrated transfer evidence: **68%**;
- minimum intent confidence: **68%**;
- maximum support: **1**;
- no manual continuation.

These thresholds exceed B1.

Starting the B2 benchmark also performs a live P27 corpus check. If B2 promotion coverage or structural integrity is not clean, the benchmark remains locked even though its specification exists.

## P29 B2 maintenance

P29 now tracks B2 exactly like the lower benchmarked levels:

- delayed consolidation;
- 30-day maintenance;
- context-variant diversity;
- failed-retest requalification;
- stale-evidence semantics;
- 60-day long-term transfer.

No special shortcut is introduced for B2.

## P30 audit

P30 requires at least:

| Resource | Required |
| --- | ---: |
| B2 readings | 6 |
| B2 listening items | 6 |
| B2 conversation scenarios | 5 |
| B2 functional missions | 2 |
| Contexts per advanced function | 2 |

The P30 runtime audit also requires:

- clean P27 B2 structural integrity;
- complete P27 B2 promotion coverage;
- a valid P28 B2 benchmark;
- B2 thresholds stricter than B1;
- only B2 missions in the B2 benchmark;
- P29 B2 maintenance support.

A dedicated Progress panel exposes these structural checks separately from learner performance.
