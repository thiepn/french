## Honest limitations

- U35 is *not* a real-world source search engine or additional licensed corpus. It does not claim to know the truth beyond the original historical lesson witness.
- Two actual utterances in one fictional-original dialogue are still **one authored dialogue**, not independent publications. A letter or notice in source material is an attributed document, not necessarily objective factual proof.
- A hypothetical adversarial speaker or editor is explicitly teaching fiction. New U35 French repair examples are original authored explanations, **never** represented as words from historical dialogue.
- Page-local memory makes drafts recoverable **only while the component remains mounted**; browser reload, route unmount or page closure discards them. No silent private text, audio or voice storage is introduced.
- A narrow key match does not assess alternate correct French expression, writing quality, understanding, native mastery, pronunciation or CEFR level.
- Text audio preparation has no microphone capture, synthetic official test, speech-to-text score or human recorded assessment.
