## Genuine gaps found and repaired directly in original sources
- **Original question-to-audio continuity:** native A1 Listen previously rendered 60 question prompts, two or three per original A1-01–24 lesson, without showing or directly replaying the particular dialogue turns supporting a question. Learners could listen to the full original dialogue or manually choose a turn by speaker but could not identify the evidence efficiently. New `originalA1ListeningEvidence` maps **all 60 original listening-question IDs to exact 1–3 sequential original French dialogue turns**, authored directly in original `u7-listening.ts`. This mapping is attached to existing source IDs only; it is not a new question or shadow course. Listen now offers a contextual **Replay source exchange** using the *same* existing browser/device TTS speech synthesizer and **Read source exchange** which reveals the exact original French lines with correct speakers, even without TTS. It neither changes options/answers nor creates mastery evidence.
- **Overreliance on questionable synthetic phonology:** U44/U45 offered 48 A1 sound contrasts but implied all could be distinguished by listening. The two focus-word homophone cases `a1-05` present *parle/parlent* and `a1-06` present *où/ou* are now explicitly marked as homophones in their existing metadata and native UI. The learner is told that orthography and meaning, not device audio, must resolve those forms. Learners may use read-first instead of listen-first. IPA remains a reference focus, **not** a measurement or validated native voice.
- **Missing independent use:** after two original sound comparisons per lesson, A1 had no substantial structured different-context speaking outcome. Added **24 optional original practical transfer prompts**, one per existing A1-01–24, with concrete new audience/situation and self-check guidance. They address greeting/register, food and quantities, family, shop description, transit, booking, invitation uncertainty, postal service and changing plans; the native Listen section reveals the checklist only when the learner chooses. No script recorder, extra store, grading, fake human audio or new studio.
- **Leaking canonical explanation on incorrect first choice:** original Listen always appended the complete original explanation after a wrong choice, often giving the correct answer immediately. U46 retains exact `checkPracticeAnswer` and the existing 48 U44/U45 wrong-option-specific misconception explanations, but now provides source replay / alternative feedback first and reveals the full canonical answer rationale only if the learner chooses it after an attempt. Correct responses continue to show the normal original explanation.
- **Actual original source defect:** historical A1-22 reading exercise presented a Monday meeting as completely finalized even though Farah still asks whether noon works. Corrected prompt and explanation to say the pair **discusses Monday**, preserving the original answer `lundi`, task ID and choice options. Clarified the repetitive A1-08 second listening question into a new practical paint-choice context, preserving source key `bleue`, exact historical listening ID and options.
- **Responsive practical interaction:** original `unified-u7.css` extended with mobile-reflowing, ≥44px touch controls, keyboard-visible focus, source transcript disclosure and optional one-shot checklist. Course/Listen remains the existing six-step lesson experience; nothing is mounted on another route.
