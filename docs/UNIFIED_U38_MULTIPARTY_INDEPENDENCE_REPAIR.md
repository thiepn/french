## Historical authority, privacy and quality boundaries

- \`originalU38Independence\` only returns when historical original U23/U37 source-qualified material exists; quoted original French witnesses are never generated from roleplay prompts.
- The 13 scenarios are separately authored new simulated situations. Even when they closely reflect a historical original passage, no simulated agreement, contact, legal right, booking, budget allocation or factual approval is presented as real.
- Source references indicate **attribution**, not automatic factual validity or independence. Two turns of one original dialogue remain **one** authored dialogue, never two independent publications.
- U38 authored-key comparisons cannot evaluate arbitrary French writing, pronunciation, fluency, genuine comprehension mastery or CEFR competence. User-supplied text and interpretations remain self-reviewed.
- Listening is the existing original text/TTS facility, **not** an asserted human audio recording; U38 captures no voice and claims no spoken proficiency.
- Page-local drafts survive tab/role/genre switching only while mounted. Reload, navigation away or unmount discards them. Original U24 opt-in navigation and native learner-state authorities remain unchanged. No silent IndexedDB/cloud/private audio or draft persistence.
- No new authoritative original source registry, second scheduler, grading engine, Account write path, CEFR certificate, CEFR automatic promotion or invented historical references.
- This phase does **not** certify original 244 course units, 192 readings or all source rights at runtime; it reuses prior historical originals without running restricted audits.
